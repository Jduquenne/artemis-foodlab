# Spec — Shopping and household

The Shopping view (`/shopping`; `/household` redirects to it) shows the list by aisle or by meal, and a separate household screen.

## Screen layout (issue #55)

Validated mock-up (owner, 2026-11-14): one main choice per screen, the rest in a menu. Step 1 (v6.147.0):
- **Header** (`features/shopping/components/header/ShoppingHeader.tsx`): title « Liste de courses », then the days and the remaining count below (as before); « + Article » (only when a period exists, no floating button) and « Options » on the right. On desktop (`lg:`) everything fits on one row: title block, mode, buttons.
- **Mode** (`ShoppingModeToggle`): « À la maison » (inventory) / « En magasin » (cart), two buttons with an icon, stored on the device (`useShoppingModeStore`, key `cipe_shopping_mode`, default `home`). It replaces « Complète / Manquants »: `home` shows the whole list; `store` hides the lines fully covered by stock or by « déjà pris » meals (`isCoveredAtHome`, `filterGroupedIngredients(…, mode, …)`) but **keeps checked lines**, struck through at the bottom of their aisle (`sortCheckedLast`), with « N déjà à la maison » under the aisle (`countCoveredByGroup`). Counter label: « N à acheter » at home, « N restants » in store.
- **Options** (`ShoppingOptionsMenu`, `FloatingMenu`): « Où j'achète » (Tout / Seulement <lieu> / Ailleurs, only when a place name exists; replaces `PlaceFilterControl`), « Affichage » (Par rayon / Par repas; replaces the Repas / Ingrédients tabs, local state), « Outils » (Articles du quotidien, Ordre des rayons, Prix au kilo, Copier la liste, Changer les jours → `/planning`). Choosing an entry closes the menu. A dot on the button and pills under the header (`ActiveFilterPills`, « Seulement <lieu> × », « Hors <lieu> × », « Par repas × ») show a non-default place filter or layout; a pill clears it.
- **Household items**: no longer a tab; « Articles du quotidien » opens `HouseholdPanel` full screen with a back arrow.
- Copy feedback goes through the notification banner (« Liste copiée »).

Step 2 (v6.148.0), rows and sheets:
- **Row** (`ShoppingItemRow`, ingredients and extras alike): two levels, 52 px high on phones (40 px on `lg:`): name (+ preparation), then a meta line (the recipe name or « N recettes », badges « ajouté », « à préciser », « congélo », the place name when flagged), the quantity on the right, and a chevron › that opens the detail sheet. Labels come from `shoppingRowQuantity` (`core/logic/shopping/shoppingRowLabel.ts`).
- **Tap by mode**: in `home`, a line with a quantity (not an extra) opens `StockEntrySheet` (« Il en faut X pour la période. Combien en as-tu ? », `DecimalInput`, live « Il restera X à acheter », « J'ai tout » = the needed quantity, « Je n'en ai pas » = 0, « Valider »; `setStock` now runs under `withPending` key `shopping-stock:<key>`); the marker is a dashed box, a green outline (partial stock) or a green check (covered), and the quantity reads « j'ai tout », « déjà pris » or what remains with « j'ai X » under it. In `store`, and for extras or lines without quantity in both modes, a tap checks the line (« dans le caddie », orange check); the quantity is what remains to buy (« sur X » when a stock exists).
- **Detail sheet** (`ShoppingItemSheet` on `ShoppingSheet`: bottom sheet on phones, centred card from `sm:`, closed by its × only): summary « Total · j'ai · à acheter » (`shoppingItemSummary`), « à préciser » note, freezer bags (`FreezerBagsSection`, same rules as the former sources modal), the meals (`SourceGroupRow`, check = « déjà pris »), the place switch (`PlaceToggleRow`, replaces the pin on the row), and « Modifier » / « Supprimer » for an extra. It replaces `SourcesModal`, `IngredientTooltip`, `PlaceFlagButton`, `IngredientCheckRow`, `ExtraCheckRow` (deleted).
- **Household card** in the list: same row style, a « Gérer » link opens the household screen.
- Quantities are rounded to 2 decimals in `effectiveQuantity` and `remainingToBuy` (`SHOPPING_QUANTITY_DECIMALS`, v6.153.1): scaled quantities such as 8 × 100 ÷ 3 = 266.666… used to show raw in the stock and price inputs, and « J'ai tout » saved that raw value as stock; the rounding also removes float leftovers (600 − 400 − 200 ≠ 0) that kept a line « to buy » with 0 g. The stock sheet rounds an already saved stock when it opens.
- The stock sheet and the extra deletion close only when the API call succeeded (`setStock` and `removeExtra` return a boolean); on failure the error banner shows and the sheet stays open with the entry.
- Structure (v6.149.2): `ShoppingModule` assembles data, filters and sheets; the lists are `ShoppingAislesView` (`components/ingredients/`) and `ShoppingMealsView` (`components/meals/`); the filter pills come from `shoppingFilterPills` (`core/logic/shopping/shoppingFilterPills.ts`); empty states use `ShoppingEmptyState`.

Step 3 (v6.149.0), « Par repas » layout (`features/shopping/components/meals/`), same in both modes (a check there always means « déjà pris » for that meal, through the source checks):
- **Meal card** (`RecipeShoppingCard`): the whole header is a button (check circle, recipe name, « touche pour tout cocher » / « N/M pris » / « tout est pris ») that checks every ingredient of the meal, bases included (`toggleSourceBatch`, one call); once all is taken the card folds to its header. The place shortcut stays as a pin button on the right of the header.
- **Base group** (`RecipeBaseGroupSection`): an inset box whose header (« Base · touche pour cocher toute la base ») checks the whole base; it folds when taken.
- **Line** (`IngredientRow`): same size as the aisle rows; a tap checks that meal's share, the chevron opens the item detail sheet.
- **Only lines linked to a catalogue food can be checked** (source checks are keyed by `foodId`; `useShoppingPeriodChecks.checkableKeys`). A free-text line shows no check circle and is ignored by the meal and base totals (`checkableCardIngredients`); before v6.149.1 it kept the meal « not all checked », so a second tap on the header checked again instead of unchecking.

## Shopping list model

- The list is **never stored**: it is recomputed on the client from the shopping days + the cached planning (`getShoppingListForDays`, `core/logic/shopping/` — aggregation, checks, recipe cards, grouping, clipboard, price).
- **Aisles and their order** (owner, 2026-10-15/16; API D-053, D-054): the list is grouped by **aisles** (`ShoppingAisle`: a shared ingredient category, or a custom per-account category « rayon », `useCustomShoppingCategoryStore` filled from `/bootstrap`). Order = `AuthUser.shoppingCategoryOrder` (shared slugs and custom ids) resolved by `resolveShoppingAisles` (`core/logic/shopping/shoppingCategoryOrderLogic.ts`): unknown or duplicate ids ignored, shared categories missing from the saved list inserted right after their nearest preceding category of `DEFAULT_SHOPPING_CATEGORY_ORDER`, custom ones missing appended at the end in creation order (`null` = default: shared default order then custom ones). An extra with `customShoppingCategoryId` goes to its custom aisle (`ConsolidatedIngredient.customCategoryId`), never to a shared one; groups are keyed by aisle id (`IngredientGroup.key`). « Autres » and « Articles du quotidien » stay apart, at the end (owner). Edited in `CategoryOrderModal` (`features/shopping/components/order/`, opened by the `ListOrdered` button of the toolbar, ingredients view only): `@dnd-kit/sortable` list (`SortableCategoryRow`, drag by the grip handle, `touch-none`; pointer, touch with a 150 ms delay, keyboard) plus ↑ ↓ buttons (`moveShoppingAisle`), custom aisles (« perso » badge) are managed in the same window, each action saved immediately through the API (`useCustomShoppingCategoryStore`: `createCategory` / `renameCategory` / `deleteCategory`, API first): `AddAisleForm` (label ≤ 50, Enter or ＋, disabled at `MAX_CUSTOM_SHOPPING_CATEGORIES` = 30, the new aisle is appended to the draft order), rename by tapping the label (`useInlineRename`, key `shopping-aisle-rename:<id>`), delete through `ConfirmActionModal` (key `shopping-aisle-delete:<id>`; its extras go to « Autres » and the API drops it from the stored order); the draft order is kept as ids and rendered from the current aisles, so a rename shows at once. « Ordre par défaut », « Enregistrer » = `PUT /me` via `withPending` key `shopping-category-order` (`null` when equal to the default, `shoppingAisleOrderPayload`), then `setUser` with the response; closes on success only. Allowed in demo. On 2-3 columns (tablet, desktop) groups are spread to balance column heights, so the path order reads top to bottom only in the single-column phone layout.
- Grouping key `${name}-${unit}`, translated to `foodId` only when writing. The same food can appear under several keys (two units, or both in a recipe and in its base): an API item check or source check (keyed by `foodId`, no unit) applies to **every** key of that food, and source-check writes are deduplicated by API key (`collectSourceCheckRequests`), then sent in **one** `PUT …/source-checks` call (a recipe card or base group, all or nothing).
- Check state: `useShoppingPeriodChecks(periodId, ingredients, householdItems)` loads item checks, source checks and extras per period (data from a previous period is never shown), derives `checked` / `stocks` / `freezerSelection` / `sourceChecked` with the pure `buildShoppingCheckState` (`core/logic/shopping/shoppingCheckState.ts`) and exposes the mutations; `ShoppingModule` only renders.
- Stored in the API under a `ShoppingPeriod` (`useMenuStore.shoppingDays` + `currentPeriodId`): shopping days, item checks (check + stock + freezer bags in one resource), source checks (per recipe × day × slot), extras. Contracts: `docs/api.md` § Shopping and household.
- Changing the shopping days deletes and recreates the period (checks, stocks and extras disappear with it) — kept on purpose.
- Freezer bag units must not include `Unit.NONE` (the API requires a unit).
- Pending feedback on every check (see `docs/conventions.md` § Loading feedback).

## Reference portion per person (D-040, v6.141.0)

`slotScaleFactor` takes the recipe's `portionsPerPerson`: slot persons and per-recipe persons give `persons × reference ÷ defaultPortions`, a dessert gives `reference ÷ defaultPortions`, nothing set = 1 (the whole recipe), gram overrides unchanged. Accompaniment shares divide the parts by the reference (persons stay persons). Example: 20 pancakes, 4 per person, 4 persons planned = 0.8 of the recipe (16 pancakes).

## Base ingredient in grams (D-042, v6.144.4)

A base ingredient with the unit `g` (grams of the base's cooked weight) is converted to base portions (`basePortionsOf`, `docs/spec-recipes.md`) before scaling: the base's own ingredients are multiplied by `portions ÷ base defaultPortions` as for a portion quantity, and the « bases to cook » list shows portions. A `g` ingredient whose base has no cooked weight contributes nothing.

## Ingredients « au choix » (D-033)

`aggregateSlots` splits an ingredient carrying a `foodGroupId` with `splitGroupIngredient` (`core/logic/foodGroup/foodChoiceLogic.ts`): the planned parts are shared equally between the account's profiles (`ShoppingCatalogue.profiles`, no notion of who eats yet, #23); each profile's share uses its resolved food (meal choice → profile default → recipe default) and its quantity per portion (meal choice `quantity`, else the recipe's). Shares of the same food are merged. Example: recipe 30 g / portion, 4 parts, Léa mozzarella 60 g, Tom comté → mozzarella 120 g, comté 60 g.
- Ingredients au choix **inside a base** (D-037 step 1, v6.123.0) are split the same way: the base ingredient is brought to the dish level (quantity × base portions used ÷ base `defaultPortions`), a meal choice quantity is per dish portion, shared between the occurrences of a base reached twice (`choiceShare` = occurrence factor ÷ total `portionFactor`); their sources keep `fromBaseId` next to `profileNames`. Known limit unchanged: a base inside a base is not expanded in the shopping list (only one level).
- **Accompaniments** (D-037 step 2, v6.126.0): `ShoppingCatalogue.accompaniments` (from `useAccompanimentStore`) and `profiles` with `accompanimentDefaults`. For each planned dish with applying accompaniments, the planned parts are shared equally between the profiles (as for the choices); each profile adds its resolved option for its share: a food option = `quantityPerPerson` × share (ingredient named after the food, its `unit`); a recipe option = `portionsPerPerson` × share portions of the option recipe personalized for that profile, its bases expanded one level, sources `fromBaseId` = the option recipe (or the base); « sans » adds nothing. Sources carry the profile name; when two profiles take the same option, the source line merges quantity and names. A group ingredient of an option resolved by the recipe default is « à préciser ». `aggregateBases` also lists an option recipe of kind base (portions taken, unit « portion »).

- A share of another food becomes an ingredient named after that food (`ShoppingCatalogue.foods`) with its `foodId`, so it groups with the same food from other recipes and is checked like any food; the recipe default keeps the ingredient's own name.
- A share resolved by the recipe default sets `ConsolidatedIngredient.toSpecify`: « à préciser » badge on the row (`ShoppingItemRow`) and a note in the detail sheet and « (à préciser) » in the clipboard text.
- Sources of a split share carry `profileNames` (shown in the sources sheet instead of « ×persons · base »).

## Manual items (« extras »)

Need: add a manual line (e.g. a specific cheese wanted on top of a recipe) that follows from one phone to another — hence stored in the API, on the shopping period.

- Entry point: « + Article » button in the header (`ShoppingHeader`), only when a period exists → `features/shopping/components/AddExtraModal.tsx`.
- Fields: name (required) + quantity + unit (optional, `Unit`) + aisle (« Rayon »: shared categories and custom aisles in the household's path order, `resolveShoppingAisles`, custom ones suffixed « · perso »; « Autre » if empty; sent by `extraAisleFields`: shared → `categoryId`, custom → `customShoppingCategoryId` with `categoryId: null`; `customShoppingCategoryId: null` is sent only when the extra had one) + optional link to a catalogue food (`foodId`, auto-fills category/unit) + optional link to a recipe planned this period (sent as uuid via `getIdByCode`). Edit mode when `extra != null`.
- Display: extras become ingredient-like lines (`extrasToIngredients`, key `extra::<id>`, category via slug → enum, no sources), merged into grouping, filtering, unchecked count and clipboard, **not** into recipe cards nor `keyToFoodId`. Rendered with an « ajouté » badge, no stock nor sources; a tap checks them in both modes; « Modifier » and « Supprimer » are in the detail sheet (delete without confirmation, the sheet closes); at the end of their aisle (`createdAt` order).
- Checking an extra uses its own `isChecked` (`PUT` extra), not an item check.
- Not shown in the "meals" view (v1). Only ingredient categories (no household category).
- A link (`foodId` / `categoryId` / `recipeId`) can come back `null` (`SetNull` on delete); an extra without category goes to « Autre ».

## Particular shopping place (D-039, API D-061, issue #39)

The account may name **one particular place** where some items are bought (`AuthUser.shoppingPlaceName`, « Merris » in the owner's household); without a name the feature is invisible. Step 1 (v6.138.0):
- Setting: `AccountModal` › Profil › « Lieu d'achat particulier » (optional, ≤ 50 characters, saved with the profile through `PUT /me`; blank clears it; allowed in demo).
- Flag per **list item of the period** (not permanent): item checks (keyed by food, so every key of that food: two units, base and recipe) and extras carry `atPlace`. `buildShoppingCheckState` exposes `atPlace: Set<ingredientKey>` (foods through `itemCheckByFoodId`, extras as `extra::<id>`); `useShoppingPeriodChecks.toggleAtPlace(key)` writes through `upsertItemCheck` (`POST` creates an unchecked row when none exists, `PUT /:id` otherwise; extras through `updateExtra`), pending key `shopping-place:<key>`; household rows are ignored. `upsertItemChecksAtPlace` (batch, `PUT …/item-checks`) is wired in the service for the recipe shortcut (step 2).
- UI (since v6.148.0, issue #55): the « À acheter à <lieu> » switch of the detail sheet (`PlaceToggleRow`) when a place name exists and the line has a catalogue food (or is an extra); a flagged line shows the place name as a badge on its meta line.
- **Filter** (step 2, v6.139.0; moved to the « Options » menu in v6.147.0, issue #55): « Tout » / the place name / « Ailleurs », only when a place name exists; local state (not persisted), forced to « Tout » when the name is cleared. `filterIngredientsByPlace(ingredients, atPlace, filter)` (`shoppingPlaceLogic.ts`) runs **before** the grouping and the mode filter, so the two combine, the « N restants » counter and the stable column layout follow it. « <lieu> » hides the household items (not concerned); « Ailleurs » keeps them. Empty states (`ShoppingEmptyState`): « Rien n'est marqué à acheter à <lieu> » / « Rien à acheter ailleurs ».
- **Clipboard** follows the filter (`buildShoppingClipboardText` gets an optional `title`): « À acheter à <lieu> » or « Hors <lieu> » as the first line when the filter is not « Tout » (`placeFilterTitle`), only the filtered groups, no household items for « <lieu> ».
- **Whole recipe shortcut** (« Par repas » layout, `RecipeShoppingCard`): a pin button on the right of the card header, shown when a place name exists: « Tout à prendre à <lieu> » flags every ingredient of the card (direct and base groups), pressed state when all are flagged (a click then unflags them all). It is a **shortcut over the food flags**, not a recipe flag: `setAtPlaceForKeys` collects the distinct foods (`placeFoodIds`, extras and unlinked lines ignored), sends them in batches of `PLACE_BATCH_MAX` = 200 to `PUT …/item-checks` (`upsertItemChecksAtPlace`), then upserts the responses into the local list; pending keys `shopping-place:<key>` per line. Consequence to know: a food shared with another recipe is flagged for both.
- **Extra form** (`AddExtraModal`): « À acheter à <lieu> » checkbox (create and edit) when a place name exists; the field is only sent in that case, so editing an extra while the feature is hidden never unflags it.

## Prices (D-043, API D-064)

Rules in `docs/decisions.md` D-043: one price per kg (per litre) per food and price store, an **indicative** estimate only, nothing shown about the amount paid. Data: `usePriceStore` + `useFoodPrices` (effective store, latest price per food).

Step #46 (v6.150.0), entry and store:
- **Detail sheet** (`ShoppingItemSheet` `priceSection` slot → `PriceSection`, `components/price/`), for a line linked to a catalogue food: title « Prix · <magasin>, <ville> », the price per kg (« / litre » for `ml`), « ≈ X € pour <quantité> » (what remains to buy, else the effective quantity; `costOfQuantity`), the price per piece when the unit goes through a unit weight (`costPerPiece`), « Mis à jour le <date> ». Without price: « Pas encore de prix ». Without any store: « Aucun magasin des prix » (+ a hint for admins).
- **Admin actions** (`useIsAdmin` and not `useIsDemo`): « Mettre à jour le prix » / « Ajouter un prix » opens `PriceEntrySheet`; the bin « Supprimer ce prix » asks `ConfirmActionModal` then `deletePrice` (`DELETE /food-prices/:id`, the previous entry is fetched back as the latest), pending key `price-delete:<id>`.
- **`PriceEntrySheet`** (no scroll): three compact rows (`PriceEntryRow`, label left, `DecimalInput` right, numbers right-aligned) in this order: Poids (Volume for `ml`, Quantité for a piece unit; prefilled with the quantity to buy), Prix payé (€, focused), Prix au kilo / au litre (prefilled with the latest price). The last two fields touched drive the third (`touchPriceEntryField`, `completePriceEntry`), shown in orange. A food without a usable conversion only shows the price per kg row. « Enregistrer le prix » sends `priceEntryPayload` (`pricePerKg` rounded to 4 decimals; `paidTotal` (2) and `purchasedGrams` (3) together or not at all) through `recordPrice`, pending key `price-record:<foodId>`; the sheet closes on success only. The props `inCart` and `footer` are ready for the « tick » entry (#48).
- **Options › « Magasin des prix »** (shown when a store exists or for admins): one radio per store (`PUT /me` `priceStoreId`, pending key `price-store-select`, then `setUser`); the checked one is the effective store (the only store when the account has none). « Gérer les magasins » (admin, not demo) opens `PriceStoresSheet`: list with rename (`PriceStoreForm`, name and city ≤ 80, `price-store-update:<id>`) and « Ajouter un magasin » (`price-store-create`). No deletion in the front (a store with prices cannot be deleted anyway).

Step #49 (v6.151.0), estimate (only when an effective store exists, « Par rayon » layout):
- **Lines to price** (`shoppingLinesToPrice`, `core/logic/shopping/shoppingPriceLines.ts`): the lines still to buy after the place filter (`isIngredientNeeded`: not checked, something remains after stock and « déjà pris »), quantity = `remainingToBuy`. Household items are not priced. A line without food link, without price or without a usable conversion is **missing**; a line without quantity (« au goût », extra with no quantity) is ignored (neither counted nor missing, no « prix ? »), since v6.153.0.
- **Row**: « ≈ X € » (`lineCosts`) or « prix ? » in amber at the end of the meta line; nothing on checked or covered lines.
- **Header**: `EstimateBadge` after « N à acheter »: « ≈ total » (`estimateCost`), amber with a « ! » when at least one line is missing (the amount is only coloured, D-043). A tap opens `EstimateSheet`: the amount (amber if incomplete), the store, « Montant indicatif », and the names of the lines left out.

Step #48 (v6.152.0), price entry when ticking:
- Device setting `askPriceOnCheck` in `useShoppingModeStore` (same persisted key `cipe_shopping_mode`), **off by default**. Switch « Saisir le prix en cochant » (`OptionsSwitchRow`) in Options › « Magasin des prix », shown to admins (not demo) when an effective store exists.
- When it is on, in **store** mode, ticking a line linked to a food, with a quantity, that was not checked yet opens `PriceEntrySheet` once the check is saved (`handleToggleItem` in `ShoppingModule`), with « Dans le caddie » and « Passer, sans prix » (closing keeps the check). The footer link « Ne plus demander le prix pendant ces courses » turns the setting off and closes the sheet. Unticking never opens it; home mode and the « Par repas » layout never open it.

## Household items

- Checked = **"to buy"** (`household-shopping-flags`), `householdService` (`getRecords` / `toggleItem` / `clearAll`). Shown in the household tab of Shopping (`features/shopping/components/household/HouseholdPanel.tsx`, category order `HOUSEHOLD_CATEGORY_ORDER` in `core/domain/household.ts`) and as « Articles du quotidien » in the ingredients list.
- Household categories from the API are not wired in the front.
